Donor Questions

Common questions answered.

How do I get my 80G receipt? +
If you donate via Give.do, your 80G receipt is issued automatically by the platform after your donation. If you transfer directly via UPI or bank transfer, email us at contact@choudhari.ngo with your name, PAN number, transaction reference, and amount — we'll issue your receipt within 3 working days.
Is my donation really tax deductible? +
Yes. Choudhari EduHealth India Foundation holds a provisional 80G certification (Ref: AAMCC7204MF20251) approved by the Income Tax Department on 18 June 2025, valid through AY 2028–2029. Provisional 80G is legally valid — you can claim 50% of your donation amount as a deduction from your taxable income under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961.
Can I donate in someone's name or as a gift? +
Yes — email us at contact@choudhari.ngo after making your donation with the name and details you'd like acknowledged. We can also send a personalised acknowledgement letter to the intended recipient if you share their contact.
Can I set up a recurring monthly donation? +
Yes — Give.do supports recurring donation setups directly on the campaign page. For direct transfers, you can set up a standing instruction with your bank. Email us and we'll help coordinate acknowledgement and reporting for recurring donors.
What happens if I donate to BloodConnect before the Give.do campaign is live? +
Use the direct bank transfer or UPI option and mention "BloodConnect" in your payment remarks. Email us with your details and we'll issue your 80G receipt and earmark your contribution specifically to the BloodConnect programme. The Give.do link will be updated on this page as soon as the campaign is live.
Can international donors contribute? +
We do not hold FCRA registration yet, which means we cannot legally accept foreign contributions at this time. We are working toward FCRA registration as a next step. If you are an NRI with an Indian bank account (NRO/NRE), please email us and we'll advise on the best way to contribute.
