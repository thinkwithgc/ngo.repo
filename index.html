Our Mission

Born from a gap that
metro India never sees.

"We built Choudhari EduHealth because the healthcare gap in rural India is invisible to those who live in cities — and that invisibility is the problem."

Established in May 2025, Choudhari EduHealth India Foundation was founded by two professionals rooted in technology and management — who grew up watching the same illness mean a routine clinic visit in the city and a life-threatening ordeal in the village. Closing that gap became our work.

In thousands of villages, a child's fever waits for a doctor who comes once a month. A mother in labour waits for blood that may not arrive in time. These are not rare tragedies — they are everyday arithmetic for rural India. We are building the infrastructure to rewrite that arithmetic: proven on the ground, measured honestly, and carried district by district until distance no longer decides who lives.

🩸
Raktify — our flagship, live now
A nationwide network of voluntary donors, ready before the emergency strikes — so that when a patient needs blood, the answer is already on its way, not hours of frantic searching. This is our primary focus for 2026.
🏥
Dr. GharoGhari — Mobile OPD, next phase
A clinic on wheels bringing free consultations, diagnostics, and medicines to villages, morning and evening. In active development — it will roll out once Raktify is firmly established and funded.
📚
EduHealth Awareness
Health knowledge that travels with our work — donor drives, school checkups, and preventive-care sessions that help communities catch illness early, before it turns dangerous.

Blood Access · National Programme
Raktify
"No patient should wait for blood."
When a patient needs blood, time is the enemy. Raktify is a living network of voluntary donors — mapped by blood group and location, ready long before the call ever comes. The moment a hospital or family raises a need, the right donors nearby are reached in minutes. No commercial middlemen, no price on a human life, no family left searching from one city to the next. Built for national scale from day one, so help is never far from anyone.

Mobile Healthcare · Planned Next Phase
Dr. GharoGhari
"The doctor who comes home"
Our next chapter. A fully-equipped medical van that reaches two villages every working day, running morning and evening shifts so daily-wage workers and farmers can be seen without losing a day's income — a qualified doctor, point-of-care diagnostics, and medicines, all free at the doorstep. The model is designed and costed; it needs dedicated funding and ground preparation before launch, and will roll out once Raktify is firmly established.
